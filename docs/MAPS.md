# Birthplace maps

`BirthplaceMaps` accepts `{catalog}` and uses `uniquePeople(personDirectory(catalog))` for both world and Korea views. Each identity counts once per filter, including linked solo careers. The pure `resolveBirthplace(person)` export supports the analysis page without a component import.

Only `birthPlace` establishes birthplace. `profileSources.birthPlace` is retained intact; wiki or missing official evidence remains marked for review. Residence and hometown are never substituted. The optional nationality overlay remains explicitly separate, retains its own source, and never acquires a region. Raw text is preserved. Exact administrative names and boundary-separated prefixes establish country/region. Bare ambiguous city/district names remain unknown. Foreign ADM1 matching is scoped to an already established birth country, including structured US states, Chinese provinces and Japanese prefectures. Korean unknown dong/district names fall back to a confirmed district/province/country. No personal coordinate/address points are generated or displayed.

## Browser globe (2026-11-17)

The default browser globe uses **MapLibre GL JS 5.14.0**, not Native. `BirthplaceGlobe.jsx` is React-lazy loaded; its CSS loads with the lazy module and the engine is dynamically imported inside the mounting effect, never initialized during SSR. Official globe example: https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/examples/display-a-globe-with-a-vector-map/ . Drag, wheel, touch zoom/rotation, keyboard and navigation buttons use MapLibre browser handlers. Style and GeoJSON are local; no map credentials, external tiles, fonts or runtime CDN. WebGL failure retains a visible message, flat-map button and region/person lists. New controls have Korean/Japanese/English/Spanish copy in `map-copy.js`.

India (`IN`) and Indonesia (`ID`) use exact country names/codes. Explicit dual citizenship is preserved; ingestion mistakes must be fixed at the source. World detail supports ADM1; Korea supports ADM3. Missing finer evidence remains at the nearest known parent; nationality-only facts stay at country level. A deeper selection does not invent a child location. Parent clicks include descendants. North Korea stays at country level and its original outline is preserved.

### Additional local assets

- `world-adm1.geojson`: 4,596 display regions, from Natural Earth 1:10m admin-1 https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces.geojson (Public Domain, same terms below). Simplified at 0.015 degrees preserving topology. Korean provinces retain the existing SGIS/admdongkor shapes. Displayed de facto boundaries are not sovereignty claims. Fetched only when ADM1 is selected.
- `korea-adm3.geojson`: 3,555 published administrative neighborhoods from the same SGIS/admdongkor 2025-01-01 source below, simplified at 0.0003 degrees preserving topology. Original `adm_cd2` identities and names retained. These are administrative-dong, not legal-dong or birth-year historical boundaries. Unmatched legal names fall back to upper levels. Fetched only when ADM3 is selected.
- `globe-gazetteer.js`: name/code-only index, 4,579 foreign ADM1 names and 3,555 Korean dong names. No personal coordinates.
- `US-adm1-metadata.json`, `CN-adm1-metadata.json`, `JP-adm1-metadata.json`: evaluated geoBoundaries gbOpen source metadata. Unused reference geometry stays outside the published site. API: https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/USA/ADM1/ , https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/CHN/ADM1/ , https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/JPN/ADM1/ . Metadata preserves pinned downloads, source licenses and represented years. US: Census Public Domain (2018); China: Wikimedia Public Domain (2019); Japan: OpenStreetMap/Wambacher ODbL 1.0 (2017). geoBoundaries requires CC BY 4.0 acknowledgement: https://www.geoboundaries.org/index.html#usage . Reference geometry is **not published, rendered or merged** into the globe, which consistently uses Natural Earth. The China reference has contested grouping and Japan carries distinct ODbL conditions. See `LICENSE-GEOBOUNDARIES.txt`, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ .

## Local assets and licensing

- `public/data/maps/world.geojson`: Natural Earth 1:110m admin-0 countries, downloaded from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson . Public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . Equal Earth projection uses `d3-geo`: https://d3js.org/d3-geo/cylindrical#geoEqualEarth . Natural Earth depicts de facto boundaries; a boundary depiction is not a claim of sovereignty.
- `korea-sido.geojson` / `korea-sigungu.geojson`: 17 province and 252 municipality/district polygons derived from https://github.com/vuski/admdongkor/blob/master/ver20250101/HangJeongDong_ver20250101.geojson . Source geometry is published by vuski/admdongkor from Statistics Korea SGIS (https://sgis.kostat.go.kr). Upstream SGIS KOGL Type 1 attribution and derivative CC BY 4.0 attribution are preserved visibly in the component. Full dataset notice is bundled in `LICENSE-KOREA.txt`; source license https://github.com/vuski/admdongkor/blob/master/LICENSE-DATA and https://www.kogl.or.kr/info/licenseType1.do . SONAR merged dong polygons by province / municipality names and simplified at 0.0005 degrees, preserving topology. This is the explicitly labeled 2025-01-01 boundary snapshot, not an assertion of current or historical birth-year boundaries. No fabricated geometry or remote runtime map CDN.
- `src/birthplace-gazetteer.js`: exact name index generated from those local assets, including ISO country aliases and explicit Korean province renamings. No guessed geographic coordinates.

The Korea source's municipality labels combine non-autonomous city/district names (for example `수원시권선구`). Text matching should normalize that administrative formatting, while preserving the original birthplace display. Country/region counts can be higher than district counts because finer locations remain unknown.
