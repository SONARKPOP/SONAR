# Artist directory expansion — 2026-11-06

Scope: boy-group debuts from 2020 onward, girl-group debuts from 2007 onward (both inclusive), the user's named female solo artists and additional female pop/K-pop performers. Earlier solo debut years are allowed, including Younha's 2004 Japanese debut. A list of every act ever formed is not claimed; unverified source links stay disconnected.

The directory and active collection are separate. Existing default 9 groups and saved preferences are unchanged. New records contain no demo videos. Dedicated artist source references are checked against YouTube API channel titles; a changed/unavailable source is paused rather than replaced by a fan/reupload channel. The original-publisher policy remains enforced.

An idol solo profile has one stable ID, artistType=solo, displayCategories=[girl,duo-solo], relatedGroupIds and relatedMemberIds. It appears in both girl-idol and solo tabs, but only once in All. Type visibility uses OR across its categories. Existing manual exclusion/allow/favorite settings remain keyed to its stable ID. A parent-group profile remains a separate selectable entry.

Videos explicitly identifying a linked solo member on their parent group's official source can link to both profiles without registering the same YouTube channel twice. Parent group selection does not automatically turn every upload into every member's solo video. A dedicated solo channel also links to its parent group. Music records remain canonical by platform/video ID, so selecting both profiles does not duplicate the card. Unrelated cover videos do not identify an ambiguous soloist.

For activity labels, a solo profile may use its parent's official source only for uploads explicitly linked to that solo profile. Ordinary new group uploads do not reset an inactive solo profile's date. Standalone solo artists appear under duo/solo; current or former idol-group soloists appear under both categories. Former membership is a search/archive relationship, not a claim of current group membership.

Dates in the new directory are curated debut/solo-career years, not inferred from channel creation dates. A first solo single, first solo EP, Japanese debut and Korean debut can differ. Fine-grained exact dates and disputed cases require further publisher review; sourceReview=needs-review is retained where official links are not established. No disbandment status is inferred from a list's end year.

Machine-readable entries and reference pages: docs/ARTIST-EXPANSION-2026-11.json.
